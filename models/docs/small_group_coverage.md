5 clips per speaker on average. Speaker SD 1.2 is a deliberately harder, assumed spread. Monte

## What this changes

- A percentile bootstrap on 10 speakers covers about 88%, not 95%, and about 81% on 5.
- The cluster t interval is closer (about 92% at 10 speakers), at the cost of wider intervals,
  but it also stays under 95%, so neither interval is exact on groups this small.
- `asr_nl` slices therefore use the cluster t interval, and groups below 20 speakers carry a
  `small group` note. Below `min_speakers` (10) they still get no number.

The speaker spreads here are assumptions. Rerun with measured variance after the first real run.
