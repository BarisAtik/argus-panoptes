# argus-panoptes 👁️

> *Argus Panoptes — "the all-seeing one" — the hundred-eyed giant of Greek myth.
> Some of his eyes always stayed open, so nothing slipped past him.*

A hobby lab for poking at AI from every angle: competing with models, breaking them,
searching through mountains of data, and tinkering with what's under the hood.

| Eye | Folder | What lives there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🏛️ The Arena | [`kaggle/`](kaggle/) | Kaggle competitions & notebooks in Python and R |
| 🗡️ The Trials | [`red-teaming/`](red-teaming/) | AI red-teaming challenges (Gray Swan arena & friends) |
| 🔦 The Watchtower | [`search/`](search/) | Elasticsearch experiments: indexing, hybrid & vector search, RAG |
| 🔥 The Forge | [`models/`](models/) | Hugging Face models: fine-tuning, multimodal (text, image, audio, video) |

## Ground rules

- Every experiment gets a short write-up: what I tried, how I validated it, and where it failed.
- Red teaming happens only in sanctioned arenas and within each platform's rules.
- No secrets, keys or private data in this repo.
